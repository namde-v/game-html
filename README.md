# Offline HTML games

Open any file in a browser. Each game is a single HTML document and runs fully offline — no images, fonts, or network requests.

| Game | File | How to play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shardfall | [games/game-001-shardfall.html](games/game-001-shardfall.html) | Arena survival shooter. WASD or arrows move, the mouse aims and fires, Shift dashes. Touch: drag the left side to move and the right side to aim. |
| Nova Break | [games/game-002-nova-break.html](games/game-002-nova-break.html) | Neon brick breaker. Move with the mouse, a finger drag, or A/D and the arrow keys. Click, tap, or Space to launch the orb. P or Esc pauses, M mutes. Clear every prism to advance. |
| Cinder Leap | [games/game-003-cinder-leap.html](games/game-003-cinder-leap.html) | Rooftop runner. Space, W, Up, click, or tap to jump — hold longer to leap farther, and jump again in the air to double-jump. S, Down, or swipe down fast-falls. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. Grab embers, avoid spikes, and don't fall. |
| Prism Well | [games/game-004-prism-well.html](games/game-004-prism-well.html) | Glass tetromino stacker. Arrows or A/D move, Up/W/X rotate clockwise, Z/Q rotate the other way, Down soft-drops, Space hard-drops, C or Shift holds. Tap or drag the well, or use the buttons. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. Clear lines before the well seals. |
| Orchid Coil | [games/game-005-orchid-coil.html](games/game-005-orchid-coil.html) | Moonlit snake. Arrows or WASD turn; swipe or the on-screen pad on touch. Eat blossoms, chain a combo, and hunt golden lotuses. Avoid walls, thorn shrines, and your own coil. P, Esc, or Space pauses, M mutes, R restarts. |
| Lantern Grid | [games/game-006-lantern-grid.html](games/game-006-lantern-grid.html) | Lantern-festival minesweeper. Arrows or WASD move, Space opens, F marks, C chords. Click, tap, or hold; double-tap a number to chord. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts. First light is safe. Three lives. Clear a grid to advance. |
| Solstice Gyre | [games/game-007-solstice-gyre.html](games/game-007-solstice-gyre.html) | Eclipse dodger. The spark rides the turning orbit; tap Left/A or Right/D (or either side of the screen) to slide into the next gate. Don't hold so long that you leave the opening. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. |
| Glint Spire | [games/game-004-glint-spire.html](games/game-004-glint-spire.html) | Moonlight slab stacker. Space, Enter, Down, click, tap, or DROP releases the slab. Match the gold guide for a perfect and a combo; overhang is cut away. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. Miss the spire and the climb ends. |
| Comet Flip | [games/game-004-comet-flip.html](games/game-004-comet-flip.html) | Pocket pinball. Hold Space, Down, S, or LAUNCH, then release to plunge. Flip with the arrow keys, A/D, Z/L, or Shift; on a touch screen use the side of the table or the LEFT and RIGHT buttons. Up, W, or NUDGE bumps the table. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. Light the rings, pop the planets, clear the shards. |
| Lumen Cadence | [games/game-004-lumen-cadence.html](games/game-004-lumen-cadence.html) | Crystals fall along four lanes. Strike each one as it crosses the glowing line with D/F/J/K or 1–4, or by tapping a lane. The run has a start screen, score/combo/level/lumen HUD, pause, game over, and instant restart. Timing grades (Perfect, Great, Good) build a combo multiplier; misses and stray taps drain the lumen as the tempo rises. Best score is stored locally, and sound can be muted. No external assets or network requests. |
| Auric Well | [games/game-004-auric-well.html](games/game-004-auric-well.html) | Auric Well — an offline forge puzzle. Slide embers, fuse matches, and reach 2048. |
| Mirage Volley | [games/game-004-mirage-volley.html](games/game-004-mirage-volley.html) | Table-tennis duel. Move with arrows, W/S, the mouse, or a finger drag. Space, J, Shift, double-tap, or the Smash button arms your next return. Thread the paddle edge, collect shards, and chain rallies. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. |
| Lumenwing | [games/game-006-lumenwing.html](games/game-006-lumenwing.html) | Dusk-canyon flyer. Space, W, Up, K, click, or tap to flap. Thread lantern gates, skim them for a gild, and collect wisps. Three wings. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. |
