# Offline HTML games

Open any file in a browser. Each game is a single HTML document and runs fully offline — no images, fonts, or network requests.

| Game | File | How to play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shardfall | [games/game-001-shardfall.html](games/game-001-shardfall.html) | Arena survival shooter. WASD or arrows move, the mouse aims and fires, Shift dashes. Touch: drag the left side to move and the right side to aim. |
| Nova Break | [games/game-002-nova-break.html](games/game-002-nova-break.html) | Neon brick breaker. Move with the mouse, a finger drag, or A/D and the arrow keys. Click, tap, or Space to launch the orb. P or Esc pauses, M mutes. Clear every prism to advance. |
| Cinder Leap | [games/game-003-cinder-leap.html](games/game-003-cinder-leap.html) | Rooftop runner. Space, W, Up, click, or tap to jump — hold longer to leap farther, and jump again in the air to double-jump. S, Down, or swipe down fast-falls. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. Grab embers, avoid spikes, and don't fall. |
| Prism Well | [games/game-004-prism-well.html](games/game-004-prism-well.html) | Falling-prism puzzle. Arrows or A/D move, W/X/Up rotate, Z/Q rotate the other way, Down soft-drops, Space hard-drops, C or Shift holds. P or Esc pauses, M mutes. Fill rows before the well crowns. |
| Orchid Coil | [games/game-005-orchid-coil.html](games/game-005-orchid-coil.html) | Moonlit snake. Arrows or WASD turn; swipe or the on-screen pad on touch. Eat blossoms, chain a combo, and hunt golden lotuses. Avoid walls, thorn shrines, and your own coil. P, Esc, or Space pauses, M mutes, R restarts. |
| Amber Lattice | [games/game-006-amber-lattice.html](games/game-006-amber-lattice.html) | Hex orb shooter. Aim with the mouse, a drag, or A/D and the arrow keys. Click, release the drag, or Space to fire. Match three or more of a color; anything that loses the ceiling falls. The trellis lowers as you shoot and if you wait. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts. Three lanterns. |
