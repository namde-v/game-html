Ridge defense

HEARTHWARD

Night brands are falling on three hearths. Bloom lantern shells from the ward-lamp and keep the ridge lit.

  • Aim and bloom — click or tap the sky. The shell flies from the lamp and opens on the pale ring. Lead the brand so it is inside that ring when the bloom opens.
  • Keyboard — WASD or arrows move the sight, Shift hurries it, Space blooms. 1 2 3 guard the left, middle, and right hearth once it flashes.
  • Brands — amber falls straight. Pink splitters break into two unless you catch them early. Pale bolides fall fast and pay more.
  • Shells refill every wave. Spares left over score points. Stop every brand and the wave is Flawless. Your blooms never snuff a hearth — only a brand that reaches the ridge can.
  • Pause — P or Esc · Mute — M · Restart — R. Leaving the window, hiding the tab, or resizing also pauses. Best score is saved on this device.
