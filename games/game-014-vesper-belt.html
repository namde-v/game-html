KEYBOARD

A D or arrows rotate. W thrusts, S retros. Space fires. Shift dashes through cinders. P or Esc pauses. M mutes. Enter launches, resumes, or restarts.

TOUCH

Use the pads. L and R rotate, GO thrusts, REV brakes, FIRE shoots, DASH phases forward. Pause and Sound stay at the top.

HOW THE BELT WORKS

Cinders split twice. Clear them to advance. Hits within a short window raise a chain, up to ×4. Cyan crystals grant one shield. From wave 2 a saucer hunts you — shoot it, or let the rocks take it, but the wave will not clear while it is still due. Every fourth wave is a squall. You have three lives. The best score stays on this device. Leaving the tab, blurring the window, or resizing pauses the run.
