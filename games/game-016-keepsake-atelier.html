Keepsake Atelier

A candlelit memory match. Pair every painted keepsake before the candle dies.

  • Cards start face up for a short peek, then close. Match every pair to clear the gallery.
  • Tap or click a card. On a keyboard, move with Arrows or WASD and flip with Enter or Space.
  • Matches feed the candle and raise a streak, up to ×8. A mismatch breaks the streak.
  • If the candle burns out you lose a lantern and it relights partway. Three lanterns end the night.
  • Clearing a gallery restores a lantern. A flawless gallery pays extra. Later galleries are larger, then tighter.
  • P or Esc pauses. M mutes. After game over, R restarts instantly. Leaving the window pauses the run.
