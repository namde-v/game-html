CINDER LEAP

You are a cinder racing the ruined roofs. Leap the gaps, snatch embers, and do not let the dark catch you.

  • Jump — Space W ↑, the JUMP button, or tap and hold anywhere. Hold longer to leap farther.
  • Double jump — jump again once in the air. Use it for high teal embers and late saves.
  • Fast-fall — S ↓, the FALL button, or swipe down.
  • Pause — P or Esc · Mute — M · Restart — R
  • Spikes and long falls cost a life. Three lives. Combos build when embers are chained. Your best score is saved on this device.
