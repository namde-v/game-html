Best 0

  • Move — Arrows or WASD. On touch, use the pad or drag inside the garden.
  • Fire — Space or J, or the Fire button. Tap the garden to loose one spark. Only one spark flies at a time.
  • The march — a briar of blossoms crawls the grove. Shoot every blossom. The head pays more. A chain of hits raises the multiplier up to ×8.
  • Toads — pale toads block the way. Four hits uproot one. A scorpion poisons them; a poisoned toad sends the briar diving straight down.
  • Critters — the spider hunts your rows and eats toads. Fleas fall and replant the grove. Scorpions cross the upper beds.
  • Lanterns — three lives. Touching the march, the spider, or a flea breaks one. Every 12,000 points mends a lantern, up to six.
  • Nights — clear the march to advance. Every third night a gilded head is worth triple. Pause with P or Esc. M mutes. R restarts. Leaving the window pauses the night.
