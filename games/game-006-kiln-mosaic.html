KILN MOSAIC

Stamp clay onto the grate. Fill a row or a column and the kiln fires it clear.

Best 0
  • Touch — drag a stamp from the tray onto the grate. Or tap a stamp, then tap where it should land. The stamp centers on your finger.
  • Keyboard — 1 2 3 choose a stamp, Arrows or WASD move it, Space or Enter stamps it. Stamps do not rotate.
  • Score — 10 per tile. A firing scores 100 × lines × lines × Fire. One line is 100 × Fire, two lines 400 × Fire, three 900 × Fire.
  • Fire climbs by one when a stamp fires a line, up to ×8, and resets if a stamp fires nothing. From Heat 2, one stamp in each set is gilt; each gilt tile in a firing adds 250.
  • Heat rises every 700 points and deals larger stamps. One smaller stamp is always in the set. Use all three to draw the next set. If every stamp still in the tray has nowhere to land, the kiln goes cold.
  • Pause — P Esc or the Pause button · Mute — M · Restart — R from pause or the kiln-cold screen.
  • Leaving the window, hiding the tab, or resizing pauses the run. Your best score is saved on this device.
