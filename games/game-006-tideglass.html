Moon pool

TIDEGLASS

Draw a glass seam across open water. When it finishes, the side with fewer glassfish is sealed. A tie seals the smaller side, and any fish caught there are swallowed. Reach the gold tide mark to open the next pool.

  • Move the needle — Arrows or WASD. On a mouse, hover the pool. On touch, drag inside the pool or use the pad.
  • Weave — Space or Enter, click the pool, or the Weave button. The seam runs both ways until it hits glass or the rim. A fish that strikes a growing seam snaps it and costs a life.
  • Turn the seam — Q E, right-click, or Turn. Horizontal ═ or vertical ║. The ghost line shows the cut. It blushes coral when a fish is close.
  • Quiet cuts score the most. Trap a lone fish against a larger school and the tide swallows it. The last fish always stays — keep sealing empty water until you reach the mark. The school also quickens as the tide rises.
  • Pause — P Esc · Mute — M · Restart from pause or the end with R
  • Leaving the window, hiding the tab, or resizing pauses the pool. Your best score stays on this device.
