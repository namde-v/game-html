Lacquer court

CROWNLINE

Drop lacquer discs. Thread four. Outwit the violet crown.

  • Aim — mouse, finger, ← → or A D. The gold ghost marks the file. Columns wrap.
  • Drop — click, tap, Space, Enter, ↓, or 1–7. A disc always falls to the lowest open seat.
  • Crown — connect four horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. Your discs wear a gold ring. Violet discs wear a diamond.
  • Lanterns — three to a run. If Violet threads four, you lose a lantern and replay that rank. A full board with no crown replays the rank and scores nothing.
  • Ranks — each crown advances the court. Violet reads further ahead, and a win streak multiplies the score. Faster crowns score more.
  • Pause — P or Esc. Leaving the window, hiding the tab, or resizing also pauses. Mute — M. Restart — R.
  • Your best score is saved on this device.
