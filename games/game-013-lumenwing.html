Dusk canyon relay

LUMENWING

You are a paper moth above a lantern canyon. Flap through the glowing gates, skim the silk, and keep your wings.

  • Fly — Space W ↑ K, click, or tap. Each press is one wingbeat. On a touch screen, tap the canyon or the Flap button. The canyon waits until the first flap.
  • Gates score 10 × combo. A gild — threading within a breath of the lantern — adds 25 × combo. Wisps in the gap score 40 × your current combo and refresh the chain.
  • Combo climbs when you clear another gate before the bar empties, up to ×8. A hit breaks the chain.
  • Three wings. The body collides; the wingtips may graze the glow. After a hit you are silk-wrapped until you are clear of the shrine. The ground and the closed night above both end a wing.
  • Dusk rises every five gates: faster slipstream, tighter gaps. Drifting lanterns appear from dusk 3, crosswinds from dusk 4. Gusts are marked by rising teal arrows or falling rose arrows.
  • Pause — P Esc · Mute — M · Restart — R
  • Leaving the window, hiding the tab, or resizing pauses the run. Your best score is saved on this device.
