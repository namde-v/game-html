LANTERN GRID

Light every safe lantern. The numbers count mines beside them. Mark the ones that would burst, and clear the row to go deeper.

  • Keyboard. Arrows or WASD move. Space opens, F marks, C chords. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts.
  • Mouse. Click to open, right-click or hold to mark, middle-click or double-click a number to chord.
  • Touch. Tap to open, hold to mark, or use Mark mode. Double-tap a number to chord. The pad moves the ember.
  • First light is always safe and opens a pocket. You have three lives. A burst clears this level’s points and relights it.
  • A teal frame means the marks match the number. A wrong mark still bursts. Best score stays on this device.
