COMET FLIP

Plunge a comet through a pocket planetarium. Pop the planets, light the rings, and clear the shard row before the well takes it.

  • Plunge — hold Space ↓ S or LAUNCH, then release. Fill the meter past the star mark and thread the top star for a skill shot.
  • Flippers — ← A Z Left Shift and → D L Right Shift. On a touch screen, press the left or right side of the table, or the LEFT and RIGHT buttons.

  • Pause — P or Esc · Mute — M · Restart — R
  • Light all three rings, then pop a planet for a jackpot. Clear the four shards to raise the orbit — gravity and planet kicks climb, and the multiplier caps at ×8. Side channels return the comet.
