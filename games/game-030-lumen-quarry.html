LUMEN QUARRY

Peel the dark stone. Mark every buried charge. Delve until a lantern goes out.

  • Dig — click, tap, or Space / Enter on the cursor. The first dig of a vein is always a safe cavern.
  • Cursor — Arrows or WASD.
  • Flag — right-click, long-press, F, or the FLAG control. Flags mark charges. Dig will not open a flag.
  • Chord — when the flags around a number match it, double-click, double-tap, middle-click, or press C to open the rest. A wrong flag strikes a charge.
  • Lanterns — three to start. A strike defuses that charge and spends a lantern. Every third cleared vein restores one, up to five.
  • Pause — P or Esc. Leaving the window, hiding the tab, or resizing also pauses. Mute — M. Restart — R from pause or game over.
  • Every vein is logically solvable. Deeper veins are larger and denser. Your best score is saved on this device.
