HALO LOOM

stained-glass match

Swap neighboring panes into a line of three or more. Matches clear, the column drops, and new panes cascade. Reach the gleam before your threads run out.

  • Four in a line forge a beam that cuts a row or a column.
  • Five call a nova. Swap it with any pane to clear that color.
  • An L or a T forges a burst that shatters a 3×3.
  • Two tools swapped together both go off. Leftover threads pay a bonus.
  • A weave that changes nothing costs no thread. A stuck board relooms itself.

Swipe a pane, or tap two neighbors. Arrows or WASD move the reed. Space or Enter picks one up; an arrow weaves it. P pauses. M mutes. R restarts from pause or the end card.
