Aim Mouse, drag, ← → or A D

Fire Click, release a drag, or Space

Match Land on three or more of the same color. They bloom, and anything no longer tied to the ceiling falls.

Drop The trellis lowers after several shots, and also if you wait too long. The bar by the cradle is that wait.

Lanterns Three lives. When the trellis crosses the line, a lantern goes out and the lowest rows are cut away.

Queue The cradle holds the next orb. A smaller orb to the right is the one after.

Pause P or Esc. Focus loss, hiding the tab, and resizing pause too.

Mute M or SFX. Restart R any time. Enter starts, resumes, or plays again.

Touch Drag on the glass to aim and release to fire. The buttons below nudge the aim or fire.

Score Bigger blooms and falling clusters score more, and consecutive blooms raise a combo up to ×8. Clearing a lattice banks a dusk bonus. Best score is kept on this device.
