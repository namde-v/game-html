Moonlit green

NECTAR PUTT

Nine holes of miniature golf on a night-blooming green. Firm putts skip a narrow brook. Soft ones sink. The cup keeps your best score on this device.

  • Putt — pull back from the ball and release. The gold arrow is the shot, and the dotted line shows where it will die. ← → or A D rotate, ↑ ↓ or W S set power, Space or Enter putts.
  • Touch — pull back from the ball, or use the pad. A tiny pull cancels and does not spend a stroke.
  • Water returns the ball to where the shot began. A hard putt can skip a short gap. Sand grabs. Gold ribbons shove a ball that is still rolling.
  • Gates glow when a firm putt will clear them. Five over par picks the ball up so a hole cannot stall you.
  • Score — par, birdie, eagle, and ace pay more. Back-to-back scores under par raise a streak to ×1.5, then ×2.
  • Pause — P Esc or the button. Leaving the window pauses too. Mute — M. Restart from the pause or round-end card.
