FEATHERKEEL

Settle the keel. Spare the flame.

  • Rotate with A, D, or the arrow keys. On a touch screen, hold Nose Left and Nose Right.
  • Flame — hold W, Up, or Space. The Flame control under the sky does the same.
  • Land upright and nearly still, gear on a lit berth. Green lamps mean this touch would count.
  • Berths — the center bronze berth is the wide safe mark. Silver is tighter. Gilt pays the most.
  • Later nights bring crosswind, a wick that restores flame, and cinders that break a hull.
  • P or Esc pauses. M mutes. R restarts. Three hulls.
