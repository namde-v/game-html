Moonlit aqueduct

Silverlock

Turn each brass lock so the quicksilver tide can run from the glowing well to the cistern.

  • Click or tap a pipe to turn it one quarter-turn.
  • Keyboard: Arrows or WASD move the mark. Space or Enter turns it.
  • P or Esc pauses. M mutes. Blur or resize also pauses.
  • The tide primes, then runs. You may still turn pipes it has not filled.
  • A spill cracks a lantern and pulls the tide back, keeping your turns. Three cracks end the run.
  • Gems on the true current pay extra. Flawless nights raise a chain up to ×4. Every third night mends a lantern.
  • Riveted locks are already set. Slate blocks are dead stone. Best score stays on this device.
